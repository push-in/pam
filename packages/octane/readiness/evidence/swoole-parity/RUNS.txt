6. Concurrency and chaos stress (Laravel 13 app, 4 workers, 2 task workers):
   parity_stress.py --port 8180 --rounds 5          (stress-l13.json, all ok)
   Per round: 256 clients x 20 increments on one table row -> exactly 5120,
   every result distinct; 1024 concurrent cache misses on fresh keys and 1024
   on one key, 0 failed; 400 clients hanging up while Octane::concurrently()
   waits -> backlog drained in 10-11 s, 0 failed hello during it;
   200 slowloris clients for 20 s while 17-21k requests ran, 0 failed;
   memory_limit fatal in an HTTP worker (500) and in a task worker
   (TaskException), 20/20 concurrently() right after; health check ok.
   Laravel log: only the 20 intended memory_limit fatals.
   Findings on the way, root-caused:
   - First design (500 drops of 2 x 300 ms tasks) timed out a probe: 300
     task-seconds of abandoned work on 2 task workers. Requests whose client
     left keep running, as on Swoole and PHP-FPM; the scenario now measures
     the drain.
   - After a memory_limit fatal PAM called cancelHttpDispatch into the
     bailed-out engine: a second fatal "Couldn't execute method
     Pam\Internal\Runtime::cancelHttpDispatch" in the logs (4 per round).
     Fixed: no cancellation into a failed engine (the worker is recycled).
   - One run (before the fix, stress-l13-before-cancel-fix.json) had 29
     client-side failures among 2048 cache misses right after the previous
     round's OOM recycling; not reproduced in 5 rounds on the fixed build.
     With APP_DEBUG=true, Whoops still prints "http_response_code() after
     header()" when the recycled worker exits after a fatal: Laravel's
     shutdown handler re-renders the (already answered and logged) fatal;
     not emitted with APP_DEBUG=false.
